# Crossroads

Open on all four sides.

The Crossroads is the only cube in the core set whose rotation makes no
difference, which makes it the one the Nightmare can drop without thinking — and
the one most likely to close a loop by accident. Closing a loop is where the leak
rule earns its keep: if a placement would remove the last fog door, it is refused
(`docs/SPEC.md` §7).

For a Sleeper it is the opposite of a T. A T offers a choice; a Crossroads offers
three, and none of them is obviously right. That is a good place to lose time and
a bad place to be caught.

- **Connectors** all four horizontals.
- **Two intended paths**, the two straight crossings. The turns are covered by
  Corner and the T; measuring them again here would say nothing new.

Cost 1, per `docs/SPEC.md` §8. It hands the Nightmare a spare branch end and
hands a Sleeper an escape; whether those balance is a question for M0.10.
