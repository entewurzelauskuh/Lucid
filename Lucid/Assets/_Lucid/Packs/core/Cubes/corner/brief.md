# Corner

The turn. A corridor that comes in from the south and leaves to the east.

Straight and Corner together are enough to draw any flat path through the
lattice, which is what makes them the two cubes M0 cannot do without. Corner is
also the first cube whose rotation matters: the Nightmare places the same
`CubeDefinition` at R0, R90, R180 or R270 to turn any direction into any other.

- **Connectors** south and east. In the unrotated cube that is a left turn seen
  from the entrance.
- **Interior** 4 m wide and high, the same corridor section as Straight, so the
  two read as one continuous passage when chained.
- **The path bends through the centre** rather than cutting the corner, so a
  Sleeper running it at full speed has the same distance to cover as a Straight.

Cost 1, like Straight: turning is not something the Nightmare should have to
pay extra for, or every dream would be a long hallway.
